## Tools to Avoid in 2026

Not every Twitter GIF downloader is worth your time — or safe to use. Here are the red flags to watch out for:

### ❌ Tools That Ask for Twitter Login Credentials

No legitimate Twitter GIF downloader needs your Twitter username and password. Any site asking for these is either phishing for your account details or violating Twitter's Terms of Service. Close the tab immediately.

### ❌ Tools with Fake "Download" Buttons

Some downloader sites are designed around ad revenue and use deceptive layouts with multiple fake download buttons that redirect to ads, malware, or app installs. If you tap "Download" and get redirected to an unrelated page or prompted to install something, that's a fake button.

### ❌ Downloader Sites That Require Survey Completion

Sites that lock your download behind "complete a survey to unlock" are wasting your time at best and harvesting your personal data at worst. Legitimate tools never require surveys.

### ❌ Chrome Extensions with Excessive Permissions

Be cautious of any browser extension that requests access to "read and change all your data on all websites." A GIF downloader should only need minimal permissions. Check reviews carefully before installing extensions.

### ❌ Outdated Tools Not Updated Since 2023

Twitter made major API and platform changes in 2023–2025. Many older downloader tools broke and were never updated. If a tool's last update was in 2022 or earlier, it likely won't work reliably.
